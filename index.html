Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our water delivery, party orders, and payments.

Where does 10siblings ltd deliver?
We deliver across our local coverage area and surrounding districts. For parties, picnics, events, and wholesale store deliveries, our dispatch trucks cover wider metropolitan zones. Message us your street address on WhatsApp to get immediate delivery scheduling.
What is the minimum order quantity?
For home pure water delivery, the minimum order is 5 bags of sachet water or 2 dispenser jars. For parties, picnics, and get-togethers, minimum order is 3 crates of chilled drinks. Wholesale store supply starts from 100 bags or 20 crates.
How do I pay for my order?
We accept bank transfer upon order confirmation and cash on delivery for verified regular customers. Your invoice is sent directly via WhatsApp before our truck sets out.
Do you sell drinks chilled for parties and picnics?
Yes! We operate a high-capacity commercial cold room where crates of soft drinks, malts, sodas, and bottled water are chilled to sub-zero temperatures. They are delivered ready to serve your guests immediately without needing to buy bags of ice blocks.
How fast will my pure water and chilled drinks arrive?
Home and retail shop orders placed before 1:00 PM are delivered same-day within 2 to 4 hours. For large weekend parties and picnics, we recommend booking 24 to 48 hours in advance to guarantee chilled stock reserved in our cold room.
